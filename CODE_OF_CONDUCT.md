# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want this project to be a welcoming, respectful, and constructive place for everyone. Contributors are expected to:

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Focus on the technical issue rather than the person.
- Welcome different perspectives and experience levels.
- Give and receive constructive feedback professionally.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, insults, and personal attacks.

## Enforcement

Unacceptable behavior may be reported privately to the repository maintainers. Reports will be reviewed fairly and confidentially where possible. Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
